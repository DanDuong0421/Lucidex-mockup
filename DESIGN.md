## Brand & Style

### Personality & Purpose
The design system establishes an institutional, authoritative, and tamper-evident digital standard for credential verification, accreditation boards, and EdTech platforms. The core emotional signature is institutional credibility, precision, and forensic trust. It avoids decorative tech tropes, neon gradients, or artificial futuristic embellishments in favor of tactile scholarly dignity and cryptographic legibility.

### Target Audience
- University registrars, ministries of education, and institutional credential officers.
- Corporate HR directors, recruiters, and background check analysts needing zero-friction authenticity audits.
- Students and alumni presenting verifiable digital diplomas, micro-credentials, and cryptographic transcripts.

### Design Aesthetic
The visual philosophy combines **Modern Editorial Classicism** with **High-Utility Functionalism**:
- **Structured Architectural Hierarchy:** Balanced margins reminiscent of formal parchment and legal documentation, paired with dense, highly structured verification tables.
- **Monochrome & Ink Rigor:** Crisp ink blacks, surgical hairline dividers (0.5px to 1px), and deep archival blues.
- **Restraint Over Excess:** Zero glassmorphism or floating neon elements. Depth is achieved via crisp paper-like tonal stacking, precise borders, and physical validation markers (security guilloche accents, cryptographic hash tags, and archival seal stamps).

## Colors

### Palette Roles & Symbolic Anchors
- **Primary (`#1A365D` - Deep Trust Academic Navy):** Embodying institutional prestige, legacy, and official authority. Used for primary navigational bars, structural headers, high-level certificates of authenticity, and formal confirmation actions.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7` - Cryptographic Cyan):** Represents the digital layer of integrity—blockchain/merkle-tree verification nodes, cryptographic hash highlights, validated badges, and interactive digital audit links.
- **Tertiary (`#991B1B` - Official Seal Crimson):** Echoes traditional institutional wax seals, security stamps, and ministerial verification marks. Also reserved for validation state anomalies, revocation alerts, or authentic watermark seals.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` - Archival Ink Black):** Delivers uncompromised typographic contrast for all body text, metadata keys, and high-density tabular records.

### Surface System
- **Base Canvas:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for certificate cards and formal documents.
- **App & Dashboard Backdrop:** Off-White Ledger (`#F8FAFC`) to minimize glare during extended operational audits.
- **Muted Surfaces & Well Containers:** Slate Tint (`#F1F5F9`) for metadata blocks, transaction logs, and cryptographic code blocks.
- **Hairline Dividers:** Border Tone (`#E2E8F0`) providing clear boundaries without visual noise.

## Typography

### Typographic Roles
1. **Academic Display & Headlines (`Source Serif 4`):** Delivers stately academic gravitas. Reserved for degree designations, certificate names, university titles, and modal headers.
2. **Body & Form Interface (`Be Vietnam Pro`):** Ensures absolute legibility for dense multilingual academic transcript tables, Vietnamese diacritics, student records, and validation forms.
3. **Cryptographic & Verification Data (`JetBrains Mono`):** Designed for machine-readable checksums, cryptographic public keys, diploma serial identifiers, transaction hashes, and timestamp records.

### Text Treatments
- **All-Caps Academic Metadata:** Use `caption` or `label-sm` with slight positive tracking (`0.04em`) for structural tags like `"STATUS: VERIFIED ON-CHAIN"`, `"ISSUING AUTHORITY"`, or `"SECURITY AUDIT PROTOCOL"`.
- **Numerical Rigor:** Ensure tabular lining figures are enabled across tables presenting GPA, credit hours, and serial sequences.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid & Structural Philosophy
The system implements a rigid 12-column fixed/fluid grid optimized for high-information density, formal certificate documents, and institutional dashboards:
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column layout, max-width `1400px`, `2.5rem` canvas margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Central credential preview canvases maintain a standardized A4 or letter ratio (1:1.414) with strict inner padding of `2.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` margins, `1rem` gutters. Split credential-and-audit view collapses into stacked panels.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` margins, `1rem` gutters. Verification details, cryptographic hashes, and signatures reflow sequentially.

### Spacing Rhythm
Spacing relies on an 8pt base grid (`0.5rem` steps):
- Micro-spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) separates paired academic labels and values (e.g., Degree field above value).
- Medium spacing (`space-md`, `space-lg`) bounds input containers, data table rows, and cryptographic proof cards.
- Macro-spacing (`space-xl`) isolates functional verification zones (e.g., document preview versus validator signature chain).

## Elevation & Depth

### Tonal Stacking & Archival Flatness
This system deliberately limits heavy drop shadows to prevent decorative distortion. Visual depth is achieved through structural layering and high-definition borders:

- **Level 0 (Base Layer):** Dashboard canvas and application background set in `#F8FAFC`.
- **Level 1 (Surface Canvas):** Certificate containers, verification forms, and card blocks set in `#FFFFFF` with a crisp `1px` border of `#E2E8F0`.
- **Level 2 (Active Inspection / Hover):** Subtle ambient lift used exclusively for interactive document previews and verification panels:
  - `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
  - Border transitions to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Cryptographic Proof Drawers):** Strict high-clarity overlay:
  - `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
  - Paired with an ink-tinted backdrop (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)`).
- **Physical Document Metaphor:** Digital credentials feature a structural internal double-border (a `1px` primary border flanked by an inner `1px` border inset by `4px`) referencing certified academic parchment.

## Shapes

### Geometric Discipline
The shape language uses minimal rounding (`roundedness: 1`), providing an architectural, precise, and serious silhouette:
- **Default Elements (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Buttons, text input fields, table badges, and dropdown menus.
- **Large Surfaces (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Certificate preview boundaries, verification ledger containers, and credential modals.
- **Security Chips & Seals:** Cryptographic pill tags (`9999px`) are used only for status pills (e.g., "AUTHENTICATED", "BLOCKCHAIN ANCHORED") to differentiate metadata tokens from rectilinear structural content.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Button (Formal Action):** Deep Navy background (`#1A365D`), pure white text, 4px border radius, medium weight typography. Hover: `#0F2942`. Active: `#0A1C2E`. Focus: 2px offset ring in `#0284C7`.
- **Secondary / Verification Action:** Transparent canvas, 1px border in `#1A365D`, text in `#1A365D`. Hover: `#F1F5F9`.
- **Destructive / Revoke Button:** Transparent canvas, 1px border in `#991B1B`, text in `#991B1B`. Hover: `#FEF2F2`.

### 2. Status Chips & Credential Badges
- **Verified / Authentic:** Pill border in `#0284C7` (or deep emerald `#065F46`), background `#F0FDF4`, label in `JetBrains Mono` (`11px`), accompanied by an SVG checkmark icon.
- **Seal of Authenticity (Official Badge):** Dual-ring circular stamp icon incorporating `#991B1B` with serif lettering around the perimeter.
- **Cryptographic Hash Chip:** Background `#F1F5F9`, border `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A` in `JetBrains Mono` with an integrated "copy hash" micro-button.

### 3. Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Verification Input (Certificate Code / Student ID):** Height 44px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, border radius 4px. Focus state: `1px solid #1A365D` with a subtle `3px` focus ring in `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)`. Font: `JetBrains Mono` for ID search inputs.

### 4. Credential Cards & Verification Ledger
- **Credential Card:** Structured container with `#FFFFFF` ground, framed by a `1px` border `#E2E8F0`. Features an institutional header band (Navy `#1A365D`), watermark seal underlay, explicit signature slots, and an inline QR verification node.
- **Audit Trail / Ledger Table:** Strict tabular borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`), zero zebra-stripes; row hover utilizes `#F8FAFC`. All timestamp and block height values rendered in `JetBrains Mono`.

### 5. Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- Rectilinear square controls with `2px` corner radius. Checked state fills with `#1A365D` featuring a crisp geometric white checkmark.
